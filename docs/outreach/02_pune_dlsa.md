**Subject:** Request for 15 minutes: a tool that prepares the Section 479 BNSS eligibility list from a prison roster

Respected Sir/Madam,

I am Sainath Gaikwad, a software engineer in Pune. In response to the Hon'ble Supreme Court's directions in In Re:
Inhuman Conditions in 1382 Prisons (orders of 23.08.2024 and 22.10.2024) on Section 479 of the BNSS, I
have built an open-source tool that takes a prison roster in the UTRC Annexure format (charge sections, date
of custody, prior convictions) and produces, for every undertrial, the Section 479 eligibility status, the date on
which the one-third or one-half threshold was crossed, and a draft application under Section 479(1) for the Legal
Aid Defence Counsel to review and file.

The eligibility computation is deterministic and the maximum sentences are taken from a law table that cites India
Code; the tool refuses to assess any section it does not know rather than guess. It runs on an ordinary laptop and
no data leaves the office.

May I request 15 minutes with you or a member of your office to show it, and to understand the format in which
the Superintendent of Yerawada Central Prison sends the quarterly data to the DLSA, so that the tool reads it
directly? If useful, we would be glad to run it on the next quarter's list under your supervision at no cost.
