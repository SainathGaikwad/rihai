# Outreach for the Pune pilot (Days 2-10)

Order of priority, and why:

| # | Who | Why them | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prayas (TISS Field Action Project), custodial justice, Pune | They have worked inside Yerawada Central Prison on undertrials for decades; they hold anonymised case data and know the DLSA | Run 50 anonymised case files; 15-minute call |
| 2 | Secretary, District Legal Services Authority, Pune (District Court, Shivajinagar) | Prepares the UTRC eligibility list; the person the output is for | 15-minute demo; a look at the UTRC Annexure A/B format |
| 3 | ILS Law College, Pune, legal aid clinic | Students visit Yerawada; a clinic can run files and verify calls within a week | Run case files; one student to verify the law table |
| 4 | Symbiosis Law School, Pune, legal aid cell | Same | Same |
| 5 | DES Shri Navalmal Firodia Law College, Pune, legal aid clinic | Same | Same |
| 6 | Commonwealth Human Rights Initiative (CHRI), Prison Reforms Programme, Delhi | National undertrial work; could carry it beyond Pune | Feedback; data |
| 7 | Project 39A, National Law University Delhi | Research credibility; they will find the holes in the law table | Review of the rule encoding |

Rules for sending: one message per person, no BCC, no attachments on the first message (link the repo and the
sample output instead), send 9:30-11:00 IST on a weekday, follow up once after 5 working days. Log every send in
`outreach_log.csv` (who, date, channel, reply). Real case data never enters the repo; `data/rosters/` is git-ignored.

What to offer every partner: the tool runs on their machine or ours, the roster never leaves their control, every
output says "for review by counsel", and the code is MIT so they keep it whatever happens to the hackathon.
